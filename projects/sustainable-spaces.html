Sustainable Spaces shelter

A concept design for a sustainable shared outdoor shelter: a planted roof that collects rainwater, seating inside and a central pond you can look into from below.

Type
Academic, ATU Sligo
Year
2026
Tools

Sheet

The concept

A shared outdoor shelter designed for the Sustainable Shared Spaces assignment. The plan is 5 m across: four hexagon-shaped entrances lead into a central area with seating around the inside and a 3 m pond in the middle. The pond has glass underneath it, so people can look up into it from inside.

Water and vegetation

The roof is planted with grass, flowers and bushes, and also works as a water catcher. Its 8.22 m² collecting area feeds tanks that hold 10,480 litres. When the pond fills up it overflows into the sides of the structure, and a drainage system carries away any further overflow.

Materials

I specified granite for the flooring, low-carbon steel for the frame and foam for the seating, and recorded them in a materials table on the drawing.

The drawing

I produced the full A3 drawing in SolidWorks: a plan view with the main dimensions, a cross-section showing the seating and drainage, a section through the pond, and a rendered view of the planted roof.
